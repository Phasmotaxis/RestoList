\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Institutional Context and Objectives}
In contemporary educational enterprise environments, information systems frequently grapple with competing tensions: providing unified administrative governance across collegiate divisions while maintaining rigorous tenant isolation, data privacy compliance, and absolute availability.

The Enterprise Information Management System (EIMS) was conceptualized, designed, and constructed under the aegis of the Department of Information Technology at Cavite State University (CvSU) for the final capstone evaluation in DICT24 (Information Management). The operational mandate requires an enterprise-scale architecture capable of orchestrating multi-campus tenants, granular organizational administrators, and user directories with zero external backend framework dependencies.

\subsection{Problem Statement}
Mainstream web development often suffers from hyper-framework dependency and dependency bloat. Commercial systems frequently rely on heavyweight application frameworks exceeding tens of thousands of third-party files. This dependency surface creates severe operational risks in air-gapped academic evaluation environments:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Package Management Vulnerability}: Dependency on remote registries (e.g., Packagist, npm) causes catastrophic initialization failure when internet connectivity is severed during defense presentations.
    \item \textbf{Uncontrolled Fatal Disruption}: Standard framework error handlers routinely suppress critical diagnostic traces or crash completely when unexpected runtime faults occur during live panel testing.
    \item \textbf{Tenant Leakage}: Inadequately enforced multi-tenancy models risk cross-organization data contamination, violating the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (\cite{ra10173}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Research Contributions}
To address these challenges, EIMS introduces three primary architectural innovations:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item A \textbf{Clean-Room Vanilla PHP 8.3 Core} that eliminates external dependencies while enforcing strict type contracts (\texttt{declare(strict\_types=1);}).
    \item A \textbf{Dual-Driver Relational Layer} providing automated, transparent runtime failover from primary MySQL 8.0 to local SQLite3 persistence.
    \item A \textbf{Live Panel Sabotage Defense Kernel} capable of trapping notices, elevating warnings to structured exceptions, and rendering high-contrast diagnostic consoles in real time.
\end{enumerate}
